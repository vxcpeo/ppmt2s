## 2. From token trade to Item NFT

**Post**

On-chain proof, step by step:

TRF -> escrow -> burn video + metadata -> evidence hash -> admin review -> Item NFT in escrow -> 48-72h dispute -> release.

No approval, no Item NFT. After release, buyer gets the Item NFT; both sides get Trade Proof NFTs. Draft protocol.

**Image:** `burn-to-nft-graphic.png`

**Alt text:** A seven-step Keepora flow diagram: buyer pays TRF, funds enter escrow, seller submits a continuous destruction video and raw metadata, an evidence hash is recorded and an administrator reviews it, an Item NFT is minted into escrow, a 48 to 72 hour dispute window runs, and the Item NFT plus Trade Proof NFTs are released after no dispute. A failed review results in no mint.
