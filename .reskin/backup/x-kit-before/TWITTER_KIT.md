## Brand guardrails

- Use the white ground, ink and magenta palette (`#E6006F` accent, `#C2005D` accent text, `#6D6A70` muted, 22–32px radii) in the source SVGs.
- Describe Item NFTs as product credentials and transfer history; do not imply they transfer brand IP.
- Rewards are based only on realized token buy/sell fees and application fees. Do not promise APY, profit, price appreciation or guaranteed returns.
- Keepora is an independent marketplace and is not an official store or licensed channel for any collectible brand.
